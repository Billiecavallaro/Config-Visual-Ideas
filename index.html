02

The understanding gap

Building speed rockets up. Understanding stays flat.

Backs up"Now AI lets us do it ten times faster. Everyone's telling us to move faster. Nobody's asking: faster toward what?"
Where it goesEnd of your open, or the start of Ryana's "the shift." This is the problem slide. Shows the villain without naming anyone.

04

The spiral

Not a flat circle. A loop that tightens toward the right problem.

Backs up"Then do it again. And again." Each lap isn't repeating, it's converging.
Where it goesA good alternative to the plain loop diagram for the framework's "four moves" slide. Shows progress, not just motion.

06

The ring

The loop runs in the middle. Design holds the ring around it.

Backs upRyana's section: deep collaboration, co-creation, design leading the problem-solving frameworks, systems-level thinking.
Where it goesRyana's "design's new job" moment. Product, tech and design sit on the ring together, so it reads as co-created, not design taking over.
07

Strategy then vs. now

One kickoff that fades away, versus a loop that never stops.

Then: strategy as a phase
Now: strategy is continuous
Backs upRyana: "Strategy used to be a phase. A kickoff, a deck, done. Now strategy has to be continuous."
Where it goesRight on that line in the video. Probably the single most important visual for the pitch, since it is the title of the talk.
08

The loop laws

Five sticky notes slapped on screen, one at a time.

Talk to a human before you touch a tool.
A prototype is a question.
Users vote. Stakeholders watch.
Robots do the churning. Humans do the thinking.
Speed comes after understanding.
Backs upThe loop laws in the framework. The most screenshot-able slide in the talk.
Where it goesNear the end of the Config talk, before the Monday morning move. Design these to look good as a social post too.
